Getting Ready as a Form of Expression

For the girls that take a long time to get ready.
GIRLHOOD   ·   PERSONAL ESSAY

For the girls that take a long time to get ready.

As girls, the big question has always been “why does getting ready take so long?” We've all experienced being rushed, getting yelled at to hurry up, or even threatened to be left behind. To everybody else, getting ready is exactly what it sounds like, but to girls it's so much more. It's an art form. It’s not just dressing up, applying makeup, or styling your hair; it’s the time that’s being put into yourself. Getting ready is loving yourself.

Getting ready is a process, we all know that, but as girls, getting ready is an art. We don’t notice it, but the time we spend getting ready shows so much about us.

01. The Model

The Model is the girl who takes over an hour to get ready. She doesn’t finish a step in her routine until it’s perfect. She’s waking up early, probably washing her face before her shower because she knows that it’s better for her skin, and then taking a long cold shower. Now as a friend, getting ready with her means receiving random but significant tips. You guys will be doing your makeup one day, and she’ll just take over and tell you something along the lines of, “You know, if you moisturize your under-eyes before applying makeup, it'll be less likely to crease.”(Save that one, it’s true.) Honestly though, if you are her, we all love you… just maybe not when you're late but look like you just got off the runway.

02. The Type B Girl

I fear this girl is my favorite girl: the Type B Girl. Her vanity is a mess, her clothes are scattered because she was looking through them for the perfect outfit, and she might have a few dishes in her room, but it's serving a purpose… maybe. She’s most likely getting ready with music playing to drown out the chaos going on around her but its not like she minds anyway. OH... and this girl may or may not be doing her makeup sitting cross-legged on the floor in front of her mirror. These girls are simply just fun to be with. They're carefree, fun, nonjudgmental, and just somebody everyone should have in their life. And in the end, she will always make it work.

03. The Experimenter

The Experimenter treats getting ready like it's a new project every day. They're creative, always taking inspiration from different things, and, most importantly, they're the most iconic trendsetters you know. Every day, they're a new person and every day, people are interested in what they're wearing. One day, she's trying a new hairstyle and the next it might be a new lash style. She mightve been inspired by a fashion magazine, a runway look, a girl she saw on pinterest, or an idea that randomly came to her while getting ready. These girls are always exploring, and you will never know what they'll come up with next. They're the girls who make you look twice but not because they're willing to look different from everybody else. They're trendsetters, They're them.

Maybe that's what makes getting ready so personal. Some girls have a whole routine, some create a mess. We all approach it differently; somewhere in the middle of the music, the outfits, the mirrors, and the last-minute decisions, we find little ways to express ourselves.

So the next time someone asks why getting ready takes so long maybe the answer is that it's not always just about getting ready. Sometimes it's about figuring out how YOU want to show up in the world.
