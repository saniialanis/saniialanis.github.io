GIRLHOOD · PERSONAL ESSAY

Is style something we create or discover?

BY THE EDITOR   ·   GIRLHOOD

Parents usually dress their children until they’re able to do it themselves. From between the ages eight and thirteen a child starts to actually care about how they present themselves. A lot of times one doesn’t have a personal style until they’re full adults, but is style something that one finds on a random Tuesday or is it something you create? Everybody is their own person, we all have our own lives, blessings, curses, and perspectives. So why wouldn’t style take part in that too? Even fashion mavericks take inspiration from Pinterest, but what I'm getting at is that inspiration and imitation are different.

I say, personal style is something we discover by creating it. As you grow you go through phases, these phases help you in your journey of your own chic.

What is chic? Chic generally means something stylish, fashionable, and or attractive, but fashionable is a relative term, and that's the beauty of it. Making your own chic means making your unique definition for fashionable. Furthermore, that's  why it’s important to go through phases. In phases one can essentially see what fits, sticks, and feels the best. Through these phases we learn more about ourselves, and maybe even allows us to take  something away from that stage in life, like a certain neckline, texture, or maybe even a realization that you prefer more fitting clothes rather than flowy. Whatever it is, it can tell you a lot about yourself and help you with the beginning of your journey. What I'm trying to say is that you're not posing or faking a look, it is just simply finding where one feels most comfortable in oneself while also learning and growing.

Social media does not make finding style easy and instead makes it feel much like a competition, what I have to say is that it's not a race. You don't need to rush and you don't need to settle for a style that just doesn't fit you. It's a journey of individuality and expression. A lot of times when people think they found they’re style and it's not actually theirs, It's simply what's trending. Fashion itself becomes less personal and iconic when everyone is dressing the same because it fits what a random idea of what looks good. It's important to ask yourself “do i really like this or is it just what's “in” right now?” There's nothing wrong with looking for what's in until it's all you look for, one should know how to make it their own.

So, is style something we create or discover? Well, style is something we create through discovery. With every stage of life and different versions of yourself there’s also a different era, much like different seasons with a new look(every phase is a different designer with different fashion styles during different seasons. Ex. New York Spring Womenswear, Paris Spring Menswear.) You want to express your individuality? Go through the phases, change your mind, don't be embarrassed, ask yourself the important questions and become the best you. After all, you're allowed to explore and experiment without having to explain yourself to anybody. Use that liberty.
